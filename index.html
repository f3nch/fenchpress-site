FENCH Press

Your training plan, written by AI for you, then tracked and adapted on your iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac.

Copy the app's prompt into any chat AI with your goal and details, paste the plan it writes back into FENCH Press, and train week by week. Each new week is built from what you actually did. Apple Health workouts are matched to your sessions, your Apple Watch guides each run and can follow a route, and FENCH Press for Mac shows every run and week on a big screen.

No account, no ads, no tracking. Your data stays on your devices, and syncs only through your own private iCloud if you switch it on.
