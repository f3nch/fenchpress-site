Support

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Issues are public, so please don't include health details you'd rather keep private.

Getting a plan

In the app, open Settings › Plans › Plan with AI. Add your goal, dates, the days you can train and anything else the AI should know, then copy the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chat AI. When it replies with your plan, copy the whole reply and use Settings › Plans › Paste a plan from AI. With your own Claude API key you can build the plan inside the app instead.

The plan won't import

The preview lists every problem. Tap "Copy fix-up message for the AI", paste it into the same AI chat, then paste the corrected reply. If the reply was cut off, ask the AI to send the whole JSON again.

Apple Health workouts aren't appearing

Workouts are matched to sessions in an approved week, on the day they started. Check that FENCH Press can read workouts in the Health app (Sharing › Apps › FENCH Press) and that the week is approved.

Setting up the Mac app and iCloud sync

On your iPhone, switch on Settings › Backups › Sync with iCloud, then close FENCH Press and open it again. On your Mac, sync is on in FENCH Press › Settings › iCloud. Both devices must be signed in to the same Apple Account. Your plan, logs, runs and settings arrive within a few minutes; Settings shows the sync status. A copy of your iPhone's data is kept before the first sync. Want to look around first? On the Mac, "Try a Sample Plan" loads an example plan.

Routes

Import a GPX file in Settings › Routes and send it to your Apple Watch (it keeps up to five). Then attach it to a planned run on the run's page, or choose it on the Watch before you start. The run keeps all its steps and targets, and the Watch shows a map, an arrow pointing the way and the distance left, with an alert if you go off route.

Apple Watch

If a change on your iPhone (a moved run, a new week) hasn't reached the Watch, open Settings › Apple Watch › Sync with Watch now with the Watch nearby. For the best GPS, allow FENCH Press to use your location on the Watch and wait for "Ready" before starting. If a run goes wrong, share the run log from Settings › Apple Watch › Share run log when you report it. If the app stops during a run, open it again: the run carries on where it was.

A run recorded in parts

If a run was recorded as several activities (restarted after a stop, or on two devices), open one of them, tap "Stitch with other recordings…" and choose the others. They become one run; Unstitch puts them back.

Backups and deleting a plan

Settings › Backups: Back up now, or Restore an earlier backup; you can also keep copies in a folder of your choice. Settings › Plans: swipe left on a plan to delete it, or make an earlier plan current again. Back up first if you might want it later. With iCloud sync on, deleting a plan removes it from all your devices.
