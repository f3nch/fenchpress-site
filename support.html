Support

Questions, problems or ideas: open an issue on GitHub. Please don't include health details you'd rather keep private.

Getting a plan

In the app, open Settings › Plan with AI. Add your goal, dates, the days you can train and anything else the AI should know, then copy the prompt into ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or any chat AI. When it replies with your plan, copy the whole reply and use Import › Paste plan from AI.

The plan won't import

The preview lists every problem. Tap "Copy fix-up message for the AI", paste it into the same AI chat, then paste the corrected reply. If the reply was cut off, ask the AI to send the whole JSON again.

Apple Health workouts aren't appearing

Workouts are matched to sessions in an approved week, on the day they started. Check that FENCH Press can read workouts in the Health app (Sharing › Apps › FENCH Press) and that the week is approved.

Deleting a plan

Settings › Plans. Swipe left on a plan to delete it, or to make an earlier plan current again. Export a backup from Home first if you might want it later.
