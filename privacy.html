Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026

FENCH Press is a training app for iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac. There's no account, no FENCH Press server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We never receive your data. Anything that leaves your devices goes only where you choose: your own private iCloud if you switch on iCloud sync, and Anthropic if you use the optional AI coach, both described below.

What stays on your devices

Your training plans, weekly schedules, logs, notes, custom exercises, activities (with their heart rate and routes), metrics and settings are stored in the app on your iPhone, and on your Mac if you use FENCH Press for Mac. They're included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) like other app data, under your control.

iCloud sync

iCloud sync keeps your FENCH Press data the same on your iPhone and Mac. On iPhone it's off until you switch it on (Settings › Backups); on the Mac it's on, since the Mac app shows what's on your iPhone. When it's on, the data listed above is stored in your own private iCloud database, under your Apple Account and Apple's iCloud terms. We can't see or access it, and it isn't shared with anyone. Some things never sync: your AI coach key (each device keeps its own), the Health workouts waiting to be confirmed, and each device's own records of files and Health checks. Switch sync off at any time on each device; turning it off doesn't delete what's already on the device.

AI coach (optional)

The AI coach is off until you add your own Anthropic API key in Settings. The key is kept in your device's Keychain and is sent only to Anthropic, in the request header. Nothing is sent until you tap Send, and the screen shows exactly what will be sent first.

Reviews and questions: this week's sessions as planned and as logged, with durations, distances, effort and average heart rate; load, fitness and form figures; weekly totals for recent weeks; the coming sessions; your race's distance and climbing; strength trends and how the plan is built (each can be switched off); things you've asked the coach to remember; and what you type. Plan changes: your plan file (its name, goal, athlete figures such as heart rates and paces, phases and weekly sessions), the plan format guide, your request, and which week you're in.

Never sent: your name, date of birth, GPS positions or routes, raw heart-rate streams, Health records, or the key anywhere except the request header. Anthropic handles what you send under its privacy policy. Past reviews and questions are kept in the app (and synced to your other devices if iCloud sync is on).

Apple Health

Read (iPhone): if you connect Apple Health, FENCH Press reads your workouts (type, time, duration, distance, heart rate, energy, effort, swim laps and strokes, and the workout's route) to match them to your planned sessions and show them with a map and splits. If you also tap "Connect recovery data" it reads resting heart rate, heart rate variability, VO2 max, sleep and body mass to show recovery and fitness.

Write (Apple Watch): the Watch app saves the runs you record to Health as workouts, with their route and, if you give one, your effort rating. The iPhone and Mac apps don't write to Health, and the Mac app doesn't use Health at all.

Health data isn't sent to us or to any service, isn't used for advertising or marketing, and isn't shared. Workouts imported into FENCH Press become activities in the app, which iCloud sync copies to your private iCloud if you switch it on. The only figures that can go to a third party are the summary numbers listed above, and only when you send them to the AI coach. You can turn off access at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

Location

The Apple Watch app uses GPS only during a run you start, including while your wrist is down, to measure distance and pace, to show your position on a route you're following, and to save the run's route to Apple Health. The iPhone and Mac apps don't use your location. Routes you import from GPX files are kept in the app, and the ones you choose are sent to your Watch.

Apple Watch run log

The Watch keeps a short diary of its recent runs (the workout's states and errors, heart rate, how accurate the GPS was and memory, but no positions) to help put problems right. It's sent to your iPhone, stays there, and goes nowhere else unless you share it from Settings › Apple Watch.

Automatic backups

The iPhone app writes a backup of your data and settings (including coach reviews, but not your AI coach key, heart-rate streams or routes) to the Backups folder in the app's Documents, visible in the Files app and included in your device backups. It stays on your device unless you share it.

Exports and files

CSV exports, coach reports and backup files are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them. Plans, routes and activity files you import are read only on your devices. The app can also copy a prompt for you to paste into an AI chat app of your choice; what you share there is between you and that provider.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. iCloud sync uses Apple's silent push notifications to learn of changes; they carry no content.

Children

FENCH Press isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
