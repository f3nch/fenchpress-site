Privacy Policy

Effective 7 November 2026

FENCH Press is a training app for iPhone. It doesn't collect any data about you. There's no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What stays on your iPhone

Your training plans, weekly schedules, logs, custom exercises and settings are stored only on your device. They're included in your device backups (iCloud or computer) like other app data, under your control.

Apple Health

If you connect Apple Health, FENCH Press reads your workouts (type, time, duration, distance, heart rate and swim laps and strokes) to match them to your planned sessions. It never writes to Health. Health data isn't sent anywhere, isn't used for advertising or marketing, and isn't shared with anyone. You can turn off access at any time in the Health app or in iPhone Settings › Privacy & Security › Health.

AI-written plans

The app can copy a prompt to your clipboard, or share it, for you to paste into an AI chat app you choose. FENCH Press doesn't contact any AI service; what you share with that service, and how it handles it, is between you and its provider under its own privacy policy. Likewise, plans you paste back are read only on your device.

Exports and backups

CSV exports, coach reports and backup files are created only when you ask, and go only where you send them with the share sheet.

Notifications

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device.

Children

FENCH Press isn't directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date. Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
